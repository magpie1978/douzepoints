Armenia

Armenia debuted in 2006 and quickly became a strong contender, regularly qualifying for the final.

Their best result came in 2008 when Sirusho finished 4th with Qélé, Qélé.

Known for mixing traditional and modern sounds, Armenia remains a consistent presence in the contest.
