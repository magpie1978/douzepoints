Australia

Australia joined Eurovision as a special guest in 2015 and became a regular participant from 2016.

They achieved their best result in 2016 with Dami Im's Sound of Silence, placing 2nd.

Australia is one of the few non-European countries in the contest, invited due to its strong fanbase.
