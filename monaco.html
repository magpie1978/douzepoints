Monaco

Monaco competed in Eurovision from 1959 to 1979, and briefly from 2004 to 2006.

They won once, in 1971, with Séverine’s Un banc, un arbre, une rue.

Monaco has not returned to the contest since 2006, citing financial and logistical challenges.
