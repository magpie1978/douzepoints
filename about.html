About Douze Points



DouzePoints.org is a data-driven Eurovision Song Contest archive designed for fans who want to explore the contest in more depth. The website brings together performances, full shows, results, and statistics from across Eurovision history in one place.

Visitors can browse entries by country, year, or contest edition, watch performances and full broadcasts, and explore how the contest has evolved over time. The goal is to make Eurovision history easy to navigate while celebrating the music, performances, and stories behind each entry.

Explore Eurovision History

Alongside the video archive, DouzePoints.org provides interactive features such as scoreboards, rankings, and statistics that allow users to analyse voting patterns, compare results between countries, and revisit iconic Eurovision moments.

Whether you're rewatching a favourite performance, analysing how a country has performed over the years, or discovering songs you may have missed, the platform combines entertainment with data exploration.

Built for Eurovision Fans

DouzePoints.org exists to celebrate the Eurovision Song Contest and make its rich history accessible, searchable, and enjoyable for fans everywhere.

From legendary performances to detailed statistics, the site is designed as a hub where Eurovision fans can relive past contests and discover new favourites.
