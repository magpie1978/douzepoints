Belgium

Belgium is one of the original Eurovision participants, debuting in the inaugural contest in 1956.

They won the contest in 1986 with Sandra Kim’s J’aime la vie, who remains the youngest winner in

Eurovision history. Belgium has seen a resurgence since 2015, regularly reaching the top 10 with

modern and alternative entries.
