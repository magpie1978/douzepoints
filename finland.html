Finland

Finland debuted in 1961 and long struggled at Eurovision until a landmark victory in 2006.

Hard rock band Lordi shocked Europe by winning with Hard Rock Hallelujah, Finland's only win so far.

In recent years, Finland has embraced alternative and rock genres, with renewed success in 2021 and 2023.
