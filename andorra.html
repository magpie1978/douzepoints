Andorra

Andorra made its Eurovision debut in 2004 and participated until 2009, becoming the

only country to sing in Catalan. Despite its efforts, Andorra never qualified for the Grand Final.

Its best result was in 2007 when Anonymous finished 12th in the semi-final with Salvem el món.
