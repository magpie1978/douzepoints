Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 February 2026

Welcome to DouzePoints.org (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

1. Who We Are

DouzePoints.org is an independent Eurovision data and archive website.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
📧 contact@douzepoints.org

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted password
  • Promo code (if required)
  • Account ID

This information is stored securely using Supabase. We do not store plain-text passwords.

B. Usage Data

We may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Videos played
  • Favourites added or removed
  • Watch progress
  • General interaction events

C. Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. This may include anonymised IP address, device type, browser type, and pages visited.

D. Advertising (If Enabled)

If Google Ads or similar advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors may use cookies to show relevant ads and measure performance.

You can control personalised ads at: adssettings.google.com

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide user accounts
  • Save favourites
  • Track watch progress
  • Improve site performance
  • Monitor analytics
  • Prevent abuse or fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal Basis (UK & EU Users)

Under GDPR, we process data based on:

  • Consent (analytics & cookies)
  • Contract (account functionality)
  • Legitimate interest (site improvement & security)

5. Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep users logged in
  • Store session data
  • Enable analytics
  • Support advertising (if enabled)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Supabase (authentication & database)
  • Secure HTTPS encryption
  • Industry-standard hosting practices

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase
  • Google Analytics
  • Google Ads (if enabled)

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account or until legally required to remove it. Analytics data follows Google’s standard retention periods.

9. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: contact@douzepoints.org

10. Children’s Privacy

DouzePoints.org is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy
